1. QUOTATION REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------
Purchase Value             | Requirement                 
---------------------------+-----------------------------
Up to Rs. 50,000           | One quotation               
Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 2,00,000 | Two quotations              
Above Rs. 2,00,000         | Three competitive quotations

2. APPROVAL AUTHORITY
---------------------
Purchase Value                | Approval Needed                          
------------------------------+------------------------------------------
Up to Rs. 2,00,000            | Department Head                          
Rs. 2,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000 | Finance Controller                       
Above Rs. 10,00,000           | CFO approval and contract review by Legal
Above Rs. 1 crore             | Board approval                           

3. VENDOR ONBOARDING
--------------------
  * Collect KYC documents, GST registration and a cancelled cheque for bank
    verification.
  * IT and software vendors must also pass a security assessment by the Security
    team.
  * Vendors must be registered in NexProcure before a Purchase Order (PO) is raised.

4. PURCHASE ORDERS AND PAYMENT
------------------------------
No PO, no payment: invoices without a valid PO are not paid. The standard payment term
is net 45 days from invoice receipt. MSME-registered vendors are paid within 30 days.

5. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
-----------------------
Employees must not take part in selecting a vendor where they or a relative has a
financial interest. Such interests must be declared to Finance and HR.
