# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

*Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.*

- **Document ID:** HR-003
- **Department:** HR
- **Version:** 2.0
- **Effective Date:** 2026-02-01
- **Owner:** HR Business Partners
- **Classification:** Internal - All Employees

## 1. Hybrid Model

Nexora follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office on three fixed days, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are flexible, and employees may work from home on those days without prior approval.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Employees must be available for meetings during these hours wherever they work from.

## 2. Allowances and Reimbursements

| Item | Amount | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Home-office allowance | Rs. 1,500 per month | Paid to all hybrid employees with payroll. |
| Internet reimbursement | Up to Rs. 1,000 per month | Submit the monthly broadband bill in NexExpense. |
| Ergonomic setup grant | Rs. 10,000 one-time | Available after 6 months of service for a chair or desk. |

## 3. Work From Anywhere

- Employees may work from anywhere in India for up to 20 days per calendar year with manager approval.
- International work from anywhere is limited to 10 days per year and needs approval from the VP of your function and from the Security team.
- Fully remote roles need approval from the VP of the function and are reviewed every 12 months.

## 4. Security Requirements for Remote Work

- Always connect to the company VPN (GlobalConnect) when working outside a Nexora office.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN switched on.
- Lock your screen when away and never leave a company laptop in a car or unattended in public places.

## 5. Meetings

Cameras should be on for client meetings and for team meetings of fewer than 10 people. Use the Slack status to show when you are away.
