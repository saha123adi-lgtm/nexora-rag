Classification: Internal - All Employees

1. BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY
------------------------
Please bring the following documents on your joining day:

  * Government photo ID proof and address proof
  * Educational certificates (originals and copies)
  * Relieving letter and last 3 payslips from your previous employer
  * 4 passport-size photographs
  * Bank account details for salary credit

2. DAY ONE
----------
Report to the reception at your office location at 9:30 AM. HR will issue your ID card,
laptop and NexHR login. You will be assigned an onboarding buddy who will help you for
your first 90 days.

Days 1 to 5 are orientation: company overview, tools setup, team introductions and an IT
walk-through.

3. MANDATORY TRAINING
---------------------
All new joiners must complete these three courses on the NexLearn portal within 30 days
of joining:

  * Security Awareness training
  * POSH Awareness training
  * Code of Conduct training and acknowledgement

4. 30-60-90 DAY PLAN
--------------------
  * First 30 days: learn the team, tools and processes; complete mandatory training.
  * Days 31 to 60: take ownership of small deliverables with your buddy's support.
  * Days 61 to 90: deliver independently and agree your first quarterly OKRs with
    your manager.

5. PROBATION AND PAYROLL
------------------------
The probation period is 6 months. Confirmation is based on a review by your manager and
HR. During probation the notice period is 30 days; after confirmation it is 60 days.

Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Provident Fund (PF) is
deducted at 12% of basic salary, with an equal contribution from Nexora.
