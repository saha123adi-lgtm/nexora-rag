1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Nexora uses the GlobalConnect VPN client. The VPN is required whenever you work outside
a Nexora office, including from home.

2. INSTALLATION STEPS
---------------------
  * Open the Software Portal (software.nexora.example) and search for GlobalConnect.
  * Click Install. The minimum supported client version is 5.2.
  * Open GlobalConnect and enter the server address vpn.nexora.example.
  * Sign in with your SSO credentials, then approve the MFA prompt in the NexAuth
    app.
  * Wait for the status to show Connected.

3. USAGE RULES
--------------
  * Split tunnelling is disabled. All traffic goes through the company network while
    connected.
  * A session disconnects automatically after 8 hours of inactivity.
  * A single account can be connected from at most 2 devices at the same time.

4. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------
Error | Meaning                             | Fix                                                                                      
------+-------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
806   | Port 443 is blocked by your network | Try a different network, such as a mobile hotspot, or ask your ISP to allow TCP port 443.
691   | Credentials rejected                | Reset your password at pwreset.nexora.example and sign in again.                         
720   | Outdated client                     | Update GlobalConnect from the Software Portal to version 5.2 or later.                   

If none of these fixes work, raise a P2 ticket with the helpdesk and attach a screenshot
of the error.
