## 1. Contacting the IT Helpdesk

- Email: helpdesk@nexora.example

- Portal: helpdesk.nexora.example
- Hours: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. Critical (P1) outages are supported 24x7.

## 2. Password Reset and Account Lock

To reset your password, go to pwreset.nexora.example and verify with MFA using the NexAuth app. No helpdesk ticket is needed.

Your account is locked after 5 failed login attempts and unlocks automatically after 30 minutes. If you have lost your MFA device, call the helpdesk. Identity verification with your employee ID and manager's name is required.

## 3. Support Priorities and SLAs

| Priority | Example | First Response | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Company-wide outage, email down for everyone | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| P2 High | Team cannot work, laptop failure | 4 hours | 1 business day |
| P3 Medium | Single user issue with a workaround | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | Request or enhancement | 2 business days | 5 business days |

## 4. Wi-Fi

Employees connect to Nexora-Corp using their SSO credentials. Visitors use Nexora-Guest. The guest password changes every week and is available at reception.

## 5. Email and Storage

- Each mailbox has 50 GB of storage.
- The maximum attachment size is 25 MB. Use the company OneDrive link for larger files.

## 6. Common Problems

- VPN not connecting: see the VPN Setup Guide (IT-003).
- Laptop running slow: restart it, install pending updates, then raise a P3 ticket if it continues.
- Cannot print: tap your ID badge at the printer; if still failing, log a ticket with the printer name.
